Lives Transformed

Behind every statistic is a human story. Meet some of the people whose lives have been changed through our programs.

“

When HopeHand built a school in our village, everything changed. My daughter was the first girl in our family to learn to read. Now she dreams of becoming a doctor.

Amara Diallo

Senegal, West Africa

“

The clean water well transformed our community. Children stopped getting sick, women no longer walked miles for water, and we could focus on growing our farms.

Joseph Kariuki

Kenya, East Africa

“

The microloan program gave me the start I needed. I built a small tailoring business that now employs five women from my village. We are lifting each other up.

Priya Sharma

Rajasthan, India

“

HopeHand gave me the tools to start my own small business. Two years later, I am employing three people from my community.

Samuel Okafor

Program Graduate

As a monthly donor, I appreciate the transparency. Quarterly reports show exactly how my contributions make a difference.

Elizabeth Warren

Recurring Donor
